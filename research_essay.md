# Comforting The Soul Through AI Chatbots in HongKong

Did you know that in Hong Kong, one in every seven people struggles with mental health issues? However, it is estimated that about three-quarters of those affected do not seek professional help due to the high cost of services and fear of being stigmatized(Ou et al.,2023).To solve this problem, AI-powered chatbots have been introduced in this field. AI-powered remote mental health services greatly improve the effectiveness of mental health care in Hong Kong by increasing accessibility, personalizing interventions, and reducing stigmatization.

AI chatbot greatly increases the accessibility of mental health support.AI delivers instant responses and round-the-clock service, which traditional services cannot match.Traditional services such as HA outpatient clinics or NGO community mental health centers have fixed operating hours. In contrast, AI chatbots (e.g., Help Me) provide 24/7 instant responses, filling the gap in service availability outside regular office hours. Meanwhile, due to insufficient medical human resources,unable to meet the rising demand for psychiatric care, psychiatric patients in public hospitals may wait up to two years or even longer to see a doctor.(The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, 2023).On the other hand, AI chatbots have achieved a significant cost reduction of 75-99% compared to traditional psychotherapy. According to Therapy Route (2025) , Private Therapy Sessions: HK$1,800-4,600 per 50-60-minute session.On the other hand, most AI chatbots offer free or low-cost subscription services, allowing users to enjoy unlimited access during their subscription period.Therefore, using AI chat tools reduces patients' waiting time and sinificantly lowers treatment costs.

Another key benefit is the personalization of interventions. .AI with culturally adapted versions can better resonate with Hong Kong people.CUHK (2023) announced the development of a “mobile app “Pai.ACT”The first AI-driven Cantonese psychological support tool for parents of SEN children in Hong Kong” This not only demonstrates that AI chatbots can be highly customized to adapt to patients' Hong Kong cultural environment and understand potential social environmental pathogenic factors, but also shows their ability to establish targeted treatment plans tailored to children with special educational needs. Furthermore, it highlights Hong Kong's leading position in this technological field.But personalization is not just about cultural adaptation; it's also about deep emotional connection. Internationally, there's a well-known companion called Ani .Ani is a 3D anime character ,she will be like a friend, remembering the user's preferences and mood while chatting with the user. Users can cultivate relationships with her through chatting, unlocking intimacy, not like developing characters, but really like your new friends.Who doesn't want to have a friend who can sincerely confide in their troubles? Thus, Ani becomes a unique confidant for each user, calming their hearts through conversation.

When using AI chatbots, anonymity and privacy protection eliminate users' feelings of shame and self-blame. Chen et al. (2018) found that due to the influence of Confucian culture,they emphasizes that one should not vent family complaints in public,valuing face above all else. As a result, individuals tend to avoid openly discussing their mental health issues. Consequently, these patients are more likely to internalize societal stigma, leading to diminished self-worth and reduced motivation for recovery. Therefore, AI chatbots are conducive to providing a guaranteed anonymous space where private information is not leaked to others if unnecessary. Perceiving that their information is secured, patients are willing to reveal about their dilemma in-depth, thereby engaging more in mental health support.

To conclude, AI chatbots provide a dual benefit of time and cost effectiveness in mental health counseling, while making the counseling more personalized. Currently, one in every seven Hong Kong citizens has mental health issue. Such powerful tools offer a gleam of hope to them in dire straits. Yet, it is believed that AI tools cannot replicate the genuine care and warmth delivered by humans. It is still the high time for us to spread our love and concern to people around us.
