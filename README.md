# AI Chatbots and Mental Health Support in Hong Kong

An academic research essay evaluating the potential of AI-powered mental health chatbots (*Comforting The Soul Through AI Chatbots in Hong Kong*) to expand care accessibility, reduce cost barriers, deliver culturally adapted support, and mitigate social stigma.

## Contents
- `research_essay.md` — Clean Markdown text of the essay
- `research_essay.docx` — Anonymized Word document (filename and metadata scrubbed)
- `research_essay.pdf` — Clean PDF export

## Key Arguments
- **Accessibility & Affordability**: Addresses Hong Kong's treatment gap (1 in 7 experiencing mental health issues, long public psychiatric wait times, and high private therapy fees) through 24/7, low-cost AI support.
- **Cultural & Personal Adaptation**: Highlights localized tools such as CUHK's Cantonese-language *Pai.ACT* app alongside conversational companion systems.
- **Stigma Reduction**: Discusses how anonymous AI interaction lowers barriers to disclosure for individuals hesitant to seek in-person care.
- **Human Boundaries**: Concludes that while AI chatbots offer timely support, they cannot replace genuine human empathy and care.
